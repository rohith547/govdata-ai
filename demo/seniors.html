Search Unclaimed Property by Name
$49 billion sitting in state databases

States hold unclaimed bank accounts, insurance payouts, stock dividends, and more. Search your name — and the names of your parents, spouse, or deceased relatives.

Tip: Also search your maiden name, your parents' names, and any old addresses you lived at.
Also search: missingmoney.com — covers all 50 states including California ($15B) and New York ($19B)

Find a Lost Pension
Millions of Americans have forgotten pensions from old jobs

If you worked for a company that went bankrupt or merged, your pension may be held by the PBGC (Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation) — the government agency that protects pensions.

Search PBGC Pension Database ↗ DOL Pension Search ↗
What you need: Your old employer's name, approximate years worked, and your Social Security number. The search is free and takes 5 minutes.
3
Check Your Social Security Benefits
Many people claim too early and lose thousands

If you haven't claimed Social Security yet, check your statement to see your estimated benefit. Waiting until age 70 instead of 62 can increase your monthly benefit by up to 77%.

View Your SSA Statement ↗
Survivor benefits: If your spouse passed away, you may be entitled to their Social Security benefit if it's higher than yours. Call SSA at 1-800-772-1213.

Find Unclaimed Life Insurance
$1 billion+ in unclaimed life insurance policies

If a parent or spouse passed away, they may have had life insurance policies you didn't know about. Insurance companies are required to try to find beneficiaries — but many never claim.

Search NAIC Life Policy Locator ↗
Free and official: The NAIC (National Association of Insurance Commissioners) policy locator searches participating insurers for free.
5
Unclaimed Veterans Benefits
Billions in unclaimed VA benefits every year

If you or a family member served in the military, you may be entitled to benefits you never claimed — disability compensation, education benefits, burial benefits, and more.

Check VA Benefits ↗ VA Insurance Search ↗
Free help: Call 1-800-827-1000 — VA benefits counselors help you find what you're owed at no cost.
6
Unclaimed Tax Refunds
IRS holds $1B+ in unclaimed refunds each year

If you didn't file a tax return in the last 3 years, you may have a refund waiting. The IRS holds unclaimed refunds for 3 years — after that, the money goes to the US Treasury permanently.

Check IRS Refund Status ↗
Note: You need to file a return to claim the refund — even if you had little or no income. Many seniors qualify for the Earned Income Tax Credit they never claimed.
7
Forgotten Bank Accounts & Savings Bonds
$25 billion in uncashed savings bonds

Old bank accounts go to the state after 3-5 years of inactivity. Paper savings bonds from the 1950s-1990s may still be earning interest and are redeemable at any time.

Treasury Hunt — Find Savings Bonds ↗
Paper bonds never expire. If you find old paper savings bonds in a drawer, they are still valid. Redeem them at any bank or through TreasuryDirect.gov.
